        FAQ

        Questions teams ask first

        Can we keep our existing phone number?

        Yes. Customers keep calling the number they know, and outgoing calls show it too. You can also start with a new number.

        Can we choose how the agent sounds?

        Yes. Pick the voice, the greeting and the way it speaks for each agent, and change them any time without rebuilding it.

        Can it book into our calendar?

        Yes. It checks each person's real availability and books the slot during the call. Your own booking system can be connected too.

        What if a caller needs a person?

        A hand-off step transfers the caller to your front desk, and a rule can trigger it from any point in the call.

        How are outbound campaigns kept polite?

        Calls go out only within your calling hours in each contact's time zone, only to contacts who agreed, and never again after someone asks to stop.

        Which languages does it speak?

        English today. Hindi and other Indian languages are next.

        Where is our data kept?

        It can run in your own cloud, so calls and transcripts stay with you. Logins to your other tools are stored encrypted.
