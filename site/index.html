        FAQ

        Questions teams ask first

        Can I use my own phone numbers?

        Yes. Any SIP trunk works for incoming and outgoing calls, whether from a carrier or your own phone system.

        Which AI models does it use?

        The language model, speech recognition and voice are settings on each agent. You can switch provider without changing the agent.

        Can it book into our calendar?

        Yes. Cal.com is built in, with an event type per doctor or staff member. Your own booking system can be connected the same way.

        What if a caller needs a person?

        A hand-off step transfers the caller to your front desk, and a rule can trigger it from any point in the call.

        How are outbound campaigns kept polite?

        Calls go out only within your calling hours in each contact's time zone, only to contacts who agreed, and never again after someone asks to stop.

        Which languages does it speak?

        English today. Hindi and other Indian languages are next.

        Where is our data kept?

        It runs on your own servers: calls, transcripts and keys stay in your database, and keys are stored encrypted.
